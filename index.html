Why Your Ads Aren’t Making Money

You think your ad isn’t working because:

  • The offer is bad.
  • Your audience is wrong.
  • Your creative is weak.

But that’s not the real problem.

The real problem? You’re sending people directly to your DMs.

Let me show you something:

You spend ₦10,000 on Facebook or Instagram ads → get 100 clicks → send them all to your DMs or WhatsApp.

Out of those 100 people:

  • 5 reply.
  • 2 ask questions.
  • 1 buys.

So you say: “Ads no dey work.”

But that’s not true. Your ads worked. They brought traffic.

Your system failed.

Because among those 99 people who didn’t buy:

  • Some were interested but didn’t know what to say.
  • Some got distracted and forgot.
  • Some checked your profile, didn't see enough information, and left.
  • Some wanted to think about it — but you had no way to follow up.

And here’s the hard truth: People don’t always buy immediately.

The serious buyers often need time. They need to read your offer, understand what you're selling, and decide whether it’s actually for them.

They are thinking:

“Is this for me?”

“Can I trust this?”

“Is this worth my money?”

But when you send them straight to your DMs, there’s no structured offer. No clear explanation. No space to think. Just a blank chat box.

So they leave. And you lose the sale not because your product is bad, but because you gave them no proper environment to make a decision.

The way people buy is changing. In 2026, you don't always need to chase people into buying. You need to give them a place where they can understand the offer for themselves.

A clean, clear page where:

  • The offer is explained clearly
  • The benefits are obvious
  • The right questions are answered
  • Trust is built and proof is shown
  • The visitor can take their time

They read it now, think about it, come back later, and eventually say: “Yes. I want this.”

That’s the power of a landing page. Not chasing. Not begging. You create a place where “yes” can grow.
